# Chef

- Year: 2002
- Designer(s): David Morgan-Mar
- Paradigm(s): esoteric, stack-based
- Family: esoteric

## Problem It Aimed to Solve

Chef was created from a humorous thought experiment: making the source code of a working program readable as an actual cooking recipe that could literally be cooked. Programs include an ingredient list and cooking steps, with variables mapped to "ingredients" and stack operations mapped to cooking actions such as "mixing" and "pouring." The core idea of the language is introducing an evaluation axis entirely different from practicality — aiming for both correct operation as a program and (in theory) the ability for a human to actually cook it as a recipe.

## Features

- Programs are written in the exact form of a cooking recipe, with a recipe title, ingredient list, and cooking method
- Variables (values) are declared as "ingredients," with their quantity corresponding to the initial value
- Stack operations are expressed as cooking actions of putting things into and taking them out of a "mixing bowl"
- Loop processing is expressed using recipe-style phrasing such as "repeat until ~ is done"
- Multiple "ingredients" or "recipes" can be combined like subroutines

## Current Status

It is positioned as an esoteric language and is not used for practical purposes. Because its source code can genuinely be read as an actual recipe, it is introduced as a particularly humorous work even among esoteric languages.

## Hello World

In a recipe called "Hello World Souffle," published by designer David Morgan-Mar, the quantities of the ingredients directly correspond to the ASCII codes of the characters to be printed, and running it outputs "Hello world!"

```
Hello World Souffle.

Ingredients.
72 g haricot beans
101 eggs
108 g lard
111 cups oil
32 zucchinis
119 ml water
114 g red salmon
100 g dijon mustard
33 potatoes

Method.
Put potatoes into the mixing bowl. Put dijon mustard into the mixing bowl. Put lard into the mixing bowl. Put red salmon into the mixing bowl. Put oil into the mixing bowl. Put water into the mixing bowl. Put zucchinis into the mixing bowl. Put oil into the mixing bowl. Put lard into the mixing bowl. Put lard into the mixing bowl. Put eggs into the mixing bowl. Put haricot beans into the mixing bowl. Liquefy contents of the mixing bowl. Pour contents of the mixing bowl into the baking dish. Serves 1.
```
