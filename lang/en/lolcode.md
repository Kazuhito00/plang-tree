# LOLCODE

- Year: 2007
- Designer(s): Adam Lindsay
- Paradigm(s): esoteric, procedural
- Family: esoteric

## Problem It Aimed to Solve

LOLCODE arose from a joke: taking the "lolcat" meme that was popular on the internet at the time (the deliberately broken English phrasing attached to cat pictures) and turning it directly into the syntax of a programming language. A program that begins with "HAI" and ends with "KTHXBYE," with variable declaration written as "I HAS A," faithfully renders the internet meme's phrasing into code as an end in itself, and it also serves as a parody of existing languages with their earnest grammatical rules.

## Features

- The start and end of a program are the greeting and farewell words "HAI" and "KTHXBYE" respectively
- Variable declaration is "I HAS A [variable name]," assignment is "ITZ," and other bits of lolcat-meme phrasing are turned directly into syntax
- Conditionals and loops are also written with meme-like expressions such as "O RLY?" and "IM IN YR [loop name]"
- Output statements are unified under deliberately broken English expressions such as "VISIBLE"
- Despite its joking appearance, it is an actually functioning programming language with variables, conditionals, and loops

## Current Status

It is positioned as esoteric and is not used for practical purposes. As a leading example of an internet meme turned directly into a programming language, it is often introduced as an approachable gateway into esoteric languages.

## Hello World

A classic, quintessentially LOLCODE "Hello World" output program, beginning with "HAI" and ending with "KTHXBYE."

```
HAI
CAN HAS STDIO?
VISIBLE "Hello World"
KTHXBYE
```
