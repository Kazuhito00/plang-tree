# Ook!

- Year: 2003
- Designer(s): David Morgan-Mar
- Paradigm(s): esoteric
- Family: esoteric

## Problem It Aimed to Solve

Ook! was created as a straightforward joke that replaces Brainfuck's eight instructions with nothing but orangutan calls (combinations of "Ook.", "Ook?", and "Ook!"). Its name and concept are an homage to Terry Pratchett's Discworld series, in which the Librarian of the Unseen University has been transformed into an orangutan and can say only "Ook" — no practical problem-solving was ever intended. It simply assigns, faithfully and one-to-one, a three-word combination to each of Brainfuck's instructions, and the very idea of "reimplementing a minimal language in the most nonsensical syntax possible" is itself the joke.

## Features

- Syntax that does nothing more than map each of Brainfuck's eight instructions to one of eight two-word combinations of "Ook.", "Ook?", and "Ook!"
- A program looks like nothing but an endless repetition of "Ook," with virtually no way for a human to distinguish meaning
- Its semantics and execution model are entirely identical to Brainfuck's; it is nothing more than a lexical rewording
- Brainfuck programs and Ook! programs can be mechanically converted into each other
- Values humor as a parody over any novelty as a language in its own right

## Current Status

It is classified as esoteric and is not used for practical purposes. As a parody of Brainfuck, it is often cited within the esolang community as a representative "one-off joke."
